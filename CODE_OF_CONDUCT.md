# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Jalte Diye Foundation is committed to creating an inclusive, respectful, and collaborative environment for all contributors.

## Expected Behavior

* Treat everyone with respect.
* Be open to feedback.
* Encourage learning and participation.
* Communicate professionally.
* Focus on constructive collaboration.

## Unacceptable Behavior

* Harassment or discrimination.
* Personal attacks.
* Offensive language.
* Intimidation.
* Deliberate disruption of community activities.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers reserve the right to remove comments, issues, discussions, or contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Our goal is to maintain a welcoming and productive environment for everyone.
