# Jalte Diye Foundation

**Lighting lives through knowledge, technology, and community action.**

Jalte Diye Foundation is a non-profit organization committed to creating positive social impact through education, technology, research, environmental sustainability, and community development.

We believe that meaningful change emerges when passionate individuals collaborate to solve real-world problems. Through open-source projects, student engagement, research initiatives, and community programs, we aim to build solutions that benefit society.

## Our Focus Areas

### Education

Developing tools, resources, and initiatives that make learning more accessible, engaging, and impactful.

### Technology for Social Good

Building open-source software and digital platforms that address social and community challenges.

### Research and Innovation

Encouraging interdisciplinary research and innovation in areas such as artificial intelligence, data analytics, cognitive science, and educational technology.

### Environmental Sustainability

Supporting initiatives related to afforestation, environmental awareness, and sustainable development.

### Community Development

Creating opportunities for students, volunteers, educators, and professionals to collaborate and contribute toward meaningful causes.
