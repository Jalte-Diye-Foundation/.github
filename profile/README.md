## Global Peace through Social Education

Jalte Diye Foundation (JDF) is a non-profit organization dedicated to creating positive social impact through education, technology, research, environmental sustainability, and community engagement.

We believe that meaningful change occurs when knowledge, innovation, and collective action come together. Through open-source projects, research initiatives, and community-driven programs, we strive to build solutions that empower individuals and strengthen society.

**Website:** https://www.jaltediyefoundation.org

## Core Projects

### [GVan](https://github.com/Jalte-Diye-Foundation/GVan)

A technology-enabled microforest initiative supporting environmental sustainability, biodiversity conservation, and community participation in plantation drives.

### [Cogentic](https://github.com/Jalte-Diye-Foundation/Cogentic)

An AI-assisted content generation platform designed to create meaningful, educational, and socially relevant content aligned with the Foundation's mission.

### [Nurolab](https://github.com/Jalte-Diye-Foundation/NuroLab)

A research and development initiative focused on neuroscience, mental health technologies, EEG analytics, and AI-driven healthcare solutions.

### [Really Real Education](https://github.com/Jalte-Diye-Foundation/really-real-education)

An educational platform promoting holistic social education, awareness, critical thinking, and lifelong learning.

### [JalteDiyeFoundation.org](https://github.com/Jalte-Diye-Foundation/jalte-diye-foundation.github.io)

The Foundation's primary website and digital platform for outreach, communication, and community engagement.

1. Explore our repositories.

## Students, Researchers, and Interns

JDF actively collaborates with students, researchers, mentors, and volunteers through internships, practice schools, research projects, and open-source initiatives.

Participants gain practical experience in:

- Open Source Development
- Software Engineering Practices
- Research Methodology
- Technical Documentation
- Team Collaboration
- Social Impact Projects

We encourage contributors to learn by building, collaborate professionally, and share knowledge openly.
