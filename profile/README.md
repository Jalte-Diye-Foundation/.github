# Jalte Diye Foundation

**Lighting lives through knowledge, technology, and community action.**

Jalte Diye Foundation is a non-profit organization committed to creating positive social impact through education, technology, research, environmental sustainability, and community development.

We believe meaningful change happens when people learn, collaborate, and build solutions together.

## Focus Areas

* Education
* Technology for Social Good
* Research & Innovation
* Environmental Sustainability
* Community Development

## Featured Projects

### Cogentic

An AI-assisted content generation platform designed to create meaningful, engaging, and socially relevant content.

### Student Innovation Projects

Collaborative projects involving students, researchers, mentors, and volunteers working on real-world challenges.

### Community Impact Initiatives

Technology-enabled programs supporting education, sustainability, and social development.
