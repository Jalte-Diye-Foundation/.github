# Cogentic – Milestone Tasks (Due Before Monday)

## Overview

The objective of this milestone is not merely coding. The team must develop a deep understanding of Jalte Diye Foundation's philosophy and translate that understanding into meaningful visual and textual content that aligns with the Foundation's vision, mission, and objectives.

---

### Objective

Develop a comprehensive understanding of Jalte Diye Foundation.

### Activities

* Study the JDF website.
* Identify major themes and objectives.
* Identify organizational values.
* Understand target beneficiaries.

### Deliverables

* Vision summary
* Mission summary
* Core values summary
* Key areas of work
* Target beneficiaries analysis

### Output File

docs/research/jdf-philosophy.md
